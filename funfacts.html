Fun Facts About Me

A little more about my interests, personality, and the simple things that make me who I am.

🍳 Favorite Food

My favorite food is anything my father cooks for me. For me, it's not just about the food itself, but also the love and effort behind every meal.

🐈 Cat Lover

I love cats, especially white cats. There's something about their appearance and quiet charm that makes them extra special to me.

📚 Manga Reader

I enjoy reading manga and manhua. I like getting immersed in different stories, discovering interesting characters, and exploring new worlds through reading.

🎮 Hobbies & Interests

I enjoy Mobile Legends, anime, music, chess, technology, and working out. I like activities that challenge my mind, build my skills, and give me something new to learn.

🌙 Peace & Privacy

I value my peace and privacy, but I don't use them as an excuse to avoid genuine connections. I believe it's possible to protect your personal space while still welcoming meaningful relationships.

☁️ Quiet Happiness

I've learned to enjoy my own company and appreciate life's little moments without needing to announce everything. Not every good experience needs an audience to be meaningful.

⚫ Favorite Colors

My preferred colors are black, white, and gray. I like their simple, timeless, and versatile look. They match the clean and understated style I prefer.

🛠️ Always Improving

I believe growth comes from consistency, experience, and learning from mistakes. I'm focused on improving myself, developing useful skills, and becoming a better version of who I am today.

✨ Simple Things Matter

I appreciate the little things in life. Sometimes, a peaceful day, a good story, a favorite game, or a meal prepared with care can make an ordinary day feel special.
